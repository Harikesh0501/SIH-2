# 📂 Official MoSPI Sample Evaluation Documents

This directory contains authentic methodological handbooks, survey guidelines, and regulatory frameworks published by the **Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI)** and the **National Statistical Systems Training Academy (NSSTA)**.

These documents are pre-formatted for direct upload and evaluation in the **AI Assessment Studio (`/assessments`)** and the **Sankhyiki Mitra RAG Engine (`/chat`)**.

## 🎯 How to Use During Live Evaluation

### 1. In AI Assessment Studio (`/assessments`)
1. Click **Assessment Studio** in the top navigation bar.
2. Under **1. Source Material Ingestion**, drag-and-drop or select any of the `.pdf` or `.docx` files above (e.g. `cpi_manual_methodology_excerpt.pdf`).
3. Set Question Count (e.g. `5`), target Difficulty (`Intermediate`), and target Bloom's cognitive level (`Apply` or `Analyze`).
4. Click **Generate Assessment with AI**.
5. Observe:
   - Chunk extraction from official MoSPI text.
   - Bloom's taxonomy mapped questions generated by NVIDIA NIM.
   - Exact paragraph citation references and distractor rationales.

### 2. In "Sankhyiki Mitra" RAG Chat (`/chat`)
1. Click **Sankhyiki Mitra** in the top navigation bar.
2. Ask any domain-specific questions matching the document content:
   - *"What is the difference between GVA at basic prices and GDP at market prices under SNA 2008?"*
   - *"Explain the two-stage aggregation formula used in All-India CPI compilation."*
   - *"What are the sampling stages (FSUs and SSUs) in the Periodic Labour Force Survey?"*
   - *"What are the 5 core dimensions of MoSPI's Data Quality Assurance Framework (DQAF)?"*
3. Observe:
   - Bilingual responses (English or Hindi).
   - Strict citation badges referencing official clauses (`Clause 1.2`, `SNA 2008 §3`, `DQAF Principle 2`).
